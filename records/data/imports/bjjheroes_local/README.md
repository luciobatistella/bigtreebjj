# Internal discovery exports

These files are research inputs. They are not public lineage claims and must
never be rendered by the Explorer without a separate direct source and an
explicit editorial decision.

## `ibjjf_academy_link_candidates.csv`

- The IBJJF athlete profile is the source for the athlete and academy fields.
- A matching academy name is only a navigation signal for research.
- `same_academy_people_*` lists known people from the same academy; it does not
  identify an instructor or the person who awarded a belt.
- No BJJ Heroes URL, biography, or image is retained in this export.
- Every row is `research_lead` and carries
  `never_publish_as_lineage_without_direct_source`.
- Nelson de Souza Lopes is explicitly excluded by editorial instruction and
  must not be inferred as a Demian Maia black belt from academy membership.
